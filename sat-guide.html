SAT vs Qudurat / Tahsili Guide

Understand exactly which tests you need — for Saudi universities and international applications.

⚡

Aramco CPP Key Threshold

For CPP eligibility, SAT Math 630+ substitutes for Qudurat 90+. Only SAT Math matters for CPP — not the total SAT score. This is the most important number for students pursuing Aramco CPP.

📐 Qudurat (قدرات)

The General Aptitude Test administered by the National Center for Assessment (Qiyas). Tests verbal and quantitative reasoning. Score reported as a percentile (out of 100). Required for admission to all Saudi public universities. Two versions: General Qudurat and Scientific Qudurat (for engineering/science).

Score: out of 100 By: Qiyas Required: Saudi Public Unis

📚 Tahsili (تحصيلي)

The Achievement Test administered by Qiyas. Tests knowledge in specific subjects (Math, Sciences, English). Score reported out of 100. Alongside Qudurat, this is required for Saudi public university admission. Scores are weighted differently by each university.

Score: out of 100 By: Qiyas Required: Saudi Public Unis

🌍 SAT

The US College Board standardized test. Total score out of 1600 (Math 800 + EBRW 800). Accepted by international universities worldwide. In Saudi Arabia, accepted as a substitute for Qudurat at some universities (KFUPM, Taibah, Prince Sultan, etc.). The Aramco CPP uses SAT Math 630+ as a substitute for Qudurat 90+.

Score: out of 1600 By: College Board Required: International Unis

Aramco CPP Line: SAT Math 630 = Qudurat 90

This is the official equivalence used by Aramco. If you score SAT Math 630+, you meet the Qudurat requirement for CPP — even if you haven't taken Qudurat. This is why many international school students prioritize improving SAT Math over taking Qudurat.

Saudi University SAT Policies

🏫 KFUPM — King Fahd University of Petroleum & Minerals

One of the top engineering universities in Saudi Arabia and the Arab world. Accepts SAT in lieu of Qudurat for admission. Strong engineering and computer science programs. Highly competitive.

SAT Accepted Dhahran Engineering Focus

🏫 Taibah University — جامعة طيبة

Located in Madinah. Accepts SAT as a substitute for Qudurat requirements for some programs. Strong medical and engineering faculties. Significant growth in quality since 2010.

SAT Accepted Madinah Medicine / Engineering

🏫 Prince Sultan University — جامعة الأمير سلطان

Private university in Riyadh. Primarily uses SAT for admissions. American-style curriculum. Strong computer science and business programs. Popular among returning Saudi students from international schools.

SAT Primary Riyadh Private

🏫 Al-Faisal University — جامعة الفيصل

Private university in Riyadh, established in collaboration with international institutions. Uses SAT as the primary admissions test. English-medium instruction. Affiliated with global universities for some programs.

SAT Primary Riyadh English-Medium

🏫 KAUST — King Abdullah University of Science & Technology

Graduate-only research university. Accepts GRE and sometimes GMAT for graduate admissions. For undergraduate track partnerships, SAT is relevant. World-class research environment on the Red Sea coast.

Graduate Only GRE/GMAT Research University
