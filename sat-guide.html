SAT vs Qudurat / Tahsili Guide

Understand exactly which tests you need — for Saudi universities and international applications.

⚡

Aramco CPP Key Threshold

For CPP eligibility, SAT Math 630+ substitutes for Qudurat 90+. Only SAT Math matters for CPP — not the total SAT score.

📐
Qudurat — قدرات
General Aptitude Test · Qiyas
Score: 0–100 Saudi Public Unis CPP: 90+ required

Tests verbal and quantitative reasoning. Two versions: General (for humanities) and Scientific (for STEM). Required for all Saudi public university admissions. Qudurat 90+ qualifies for Aramco CPP in lieu of SAT Math 630+.

FormatVerbal + Quantitative, MCQ
Duration~2.5 hours
Frequency~4–5 times/year
Fee~SAR 100
Register on Qiyas.sa →
📚
Tahsili — تحصيلي
Achievement Test · Qiyas
Score: 0–100 Saudi Public Unis Required with Qudurat

Tests subject knowledge in Math, Sciences, Arabic, and English. Two tracks: Scientific (علمي) and Literary (أدبي). Required alongside Qudurat for all Saudi public university admissions. Each university weights it differently in their admissions formula.

FormatSubject knowledge, MCQ
Duration~2 hours
Frequency~4–5 times/year
Fee~SAR 100
Register on Qiyas.sa →
🌍
SAT
Scholastic Assessment Test · College Board
Score: 400–1600 US/Canada/Global CPP: Math 630+

Math (800) + Evidence-Based Reading & Writing (800). Recommended for US/Canadian universities. In Saudi Arabia, SAT Math 630+ substitutes for Qudurat 90+ for Aramco CPP. Accepted by KFUPM, Prince Sultan, Al-Faisal, Taibah in lieu of Qudurat.

FormatMath + Reading & Writing
Duration2h 14min
Frequency7 dates/year globally
Fee~$60 + regional fee
Register on CollegeBoard.org →
🎧
IELTS Academic
International English Language Testing System
Band: 0–9 UK / Australia / Canada IDP / British Council

The world's most popular English language test. Required by virtually all UK, Australian, and Canadian universities. Some US universities also accept IELTS. Most top UK unis require 6.5–7.0 overall with no band below 6.5. Comprises Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking sections.

FormatListening · Reading · Writing · Speaking
Duration~2h 45min
Validity2 years
Fee~SAR 900–1,000
Book IELTS Test →
💬
TOEFL iBT
Test of English as a Foreign Language · ETS
Score: 0–120 USA / Canada ETS

Preferred by most US universities over IELTS. Tests reading, listening, speaking, and writing in an academic context. Most top US universities require 100+ for competitive admissions. Some Saudi and UAE universities also accept TOEFL as an English proficiency proof.

FormatReading · Listening · Speaking · Writing
Duration~3 hours
Validity2 years
Fee~$245
Register for TOEFL →
📊
ACT
American College Testing
Score: 1–36 USA / Canada ACT Inc.

Alternative to the SAT for US/Canadian university admissions — all universities that accept the SAT accept the ACT. Four sections: English, Math, Reading, Science Reasoning. Some students perform significantly better on the ACT vs SAT due to the Science section and pacing.

FormatEnglish · Math · Reading · Science
Duration~2h 55min (no Writing)
Frequency6–7 dates/year
Fee~$68 (no Writing)

Aramco CPP Line: SAT Math 630 = Qudurat 90

This is the official equivalence used by Aramco. If you score SAT Math 630+, you meet the Qudurat requirement for CPP — even if you haven't taken Qudurat. This is why many international school students prioritize improving SAT Math over taking Qudurat.

Saudi university admissions test policies — always verify on the official admissions page as requirements change annually.
