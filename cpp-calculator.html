The CPP Journey
📋
Eligibility Check
Before applying
Verify you meet all 4 criteria: cumulative GPA 85%+, Math & Science GPA 85%+, SAT Math 630+ (or Qudurat 90+), and international school graduation.
📝
Application Window
February – March annually
Apply through the Saudi Aramco careers portal. Have your transcript, test scores, and school counselor contact ready. The window closes quickly.
🧪
Screening Tests
May 1–2
Separate registration required. Tests assess academic aptitude and English proficiency. Results determine continuation in the process.
🤝
Aramco Career Fair
June 3–4
In-person career fair in Saudi Arabia. Finalists meet Aramco representatives and formally accept the sponsorship. Dress professionally.
🎓
Orientation & University
August 2 → University begins
Orientation followed by a prep year (or direct university entry with the CPP Waiver for Top-30 admits). Aramco funding begins from this point.
