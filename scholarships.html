Aramco College Degree Program (CPP)

The most prestigious sponsorship for Saudi international school graduates

The Aramco CPP sponsors high-achieving Saudi international school graduates to study at top universities abroad. It covers full tuition, living expenses, and provides a guaranteed job at Saudi Aramco upon graduation.

SAT Math
630+ (or Qudurat 90+)
Cumulative GPA
85%+ overall
Math & Science GPA
85%+ in M&S subjects
School Type
International school graduates
Key Dates
February–March: Application window opens
May 1–2: Screening tests
June 3–4: Career fair
August 2: Orientation
CPP Waiver Pathway

If you receive an unconditional offer from a Top 30 university globally, you may apply for the CPP Waiver — skipping the prep year and entering directly. This is the pathway for students accepted to schools like MIT, Stanford, Imperial, Oxford, or any university in the global Top 30.

Other Scholarships for Saudi Students
🎓

King Abdullah Scholarship Program (KASP)

Full Funding — Tuition + Living

The Saudi government's primary international scholarship program. Covers tuition, living allowance, health insurance, and annual flights for approved programs at accredited international universities.

Government Full Funding Undergraduate & Graduate
⭐

Mawhiba Scholarship

Full Funding — For Gifted Students

For students identified as gifted by the National Center for Gifted & Creativity (Mawhiba). Supports exceptional students to study at elite international universities. Requires Mawhiba identification or excellence in national/international competitions.

Gifted Competitive Full Funding
🧪

SABIC Scholarship

Full Funding + Employment Path

SABIC sponsors Saudi students in chemistry, chemical engineering, materials science, and related fields at top universities. Like Aramco CPP, it includes a post-graduation employment pathway at SABIC.

STEM Chemistry/Engineering Employment Guaranteed
📡

STC Scholarship

Partial to Full Funding

Saudi Telecom Company sponsors students in technology, telecommunications, computer science, and information systems. Includes mentorship and an employment path at STC upon graduation.

Technology CS/Telecom Employment Path
🏛️

University Merit Scholarships

Varies — Partial to Full

Many international universities offer merit-based scholarships that Saudi students qualify for. US universities with large endowments (MIT, Harvard, Princeton) meet full demonstrated need. UK universities offer specific international scholarships.

Merit-Based Need-Based University-Specific
🌍

Vision 2030 Scholarships

Varies by Program

Various Vision 2030-aligned programs sponsor Saudi students in priority fields: renewable energy, tourism, entertainment, technology, and healthcare. Programs evolve regularly — check HRDF and relevant government ministry sites.
