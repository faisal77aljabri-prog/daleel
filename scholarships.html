King Abdullah Scholarship Program (KASP)

Full Funding — Tuition + Living Allowance

The Saudi government's primary international scholarship. Covers full tuition, monthly living allowance, health insurance, and annual return flights to the Kingdom. Available at accredited universities in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and Europe.

EligibilitySaudi nationals, strong academic record
LevelUndergraduate, Masters, PhD
CoversTuition + living + health + flights
Apply viaMinistry of Education (MoE)
Government Full Funding Undergrad & Postgrad
