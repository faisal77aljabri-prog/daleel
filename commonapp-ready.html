🇸🇦 Saudi Honors — Explained for Adcoms

US admissions officers don't always recognize these. Here's how to describe them.

🔬 KAUST SRSI

Saudi Research Science Institute — a selective summer research program at KAUST. ~Top 80 of 15,000 applicants (≈0.53%). Describe it as a nationally prestigious research selection, not a "summer camp."

☄️ Purple Comet

An international team math competition. Always frame with global context: "International Math Meet — global team competition," not a local contest.

🧪 Ibdaa (إبداع)

Saudi Arabia's national science & innovation competition. Frame as "National Science & Innovation Olympiad" so adcoms grasp the scale.

🎓 Mawhiba (موهبة)

The national gifted-students foundation. Selection signals top-percentile aptitude — describe as "National Gifted & Talented Program."

🛢️ Aramco CPP Selection

Selection for Aramco's College Preparation Program is itself an honor — one of the most competitive sponsorships in the Kingdom. Top US adcoms recognize Aramco.

Additional Information — The Most Underused Section.

Most students leave this blank. Saudi students have more to explain here than almost anyone.

📚 Curriculum Context

If your school offers an American Diploma without AP, explain it.

My school does not offer AP or IB courses. I independently enrolled in an accredited online program to pursue rigorous coursework in [subjects].

📊 Saudi Grading System

US adcoms don't know what 95% means in a Saudi context.

Saudi schools grade on a percentage scale. A score of 90%+ is considered exceptional and represents the top tier of academic achievement.

🛢️ Aramco CPP Context

If applying for the CPP waiver, mention it — it adds credibility.

I am a candidate for the Saudi Aramco College Preparation Program (CPP), a highly selective national sponsorship program for top-performing Saudi students.

🌍 COVID / Unique Circumstances

If anything unusual affected your record — school changes, moving countries, curriculum gaps — explain it briefly and factually here.

🔬 Research Explanation

If you did KAUST SRSI or similar, explain the program's prestige.

I was selected for the KAUST Saudi Research Science Institute (SRSI), a nationally competitive summer research program admitting roughly the top 0.5% of applicants.
