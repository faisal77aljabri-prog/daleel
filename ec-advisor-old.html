⚡ EC Tier Breakdown

What tier are you actually in?

🏆

Tier 1 — National / International Published research, founded a company, national competition winner, Olympiad medalist. Rare — top 1% of applicants.

⭐

Tier 2 — Regional Impact Founded a club that grew significantly, regional competition winner, organized events with real community impact. Where strong applicants sit.

📌

Tier 3 — School Leadership School club president, varsity team, consistent community service, significant volunteer hours. Average for competitive applicants.

📋

Tier 4 — Participation Club member (not leader), occasional volunteering, casual hobbies. Common — needs differentiation.

⚠️

Tier 5 — Minimal Few or no meaningful activities. For top universities, this is a significant weakness. Addressable with time.

🇸🇦 Saudi ECs That Stand Out

  • STEM Competitions: Saudi Olympiad, FIRST Robotics, Google Science Fair
  • Entrepreneurship: Startup in Riyadh/Jeddah/Medina — Vision 2030 angle
  • Research: Any published or presented research, even at school level
  • Community firsts: "First in my city/school to…" is powerful
  • Arabic writing/poetry: Literary talent in Arabic reads as intellectual distinctiveness
  • Religious leadership: Leading youth programs, Quran competitions (handled with specificity)
